Here are the habits to look for:

1. **Antithesis**: Setting up one idea only to knock it down for another. 
Example: "You can fix each of these. You cannot fix the category."
2. **Fragment**: Verbless phrases standing in for sentences, usually parallel and often in a row. 
Example: "Two mechanisms, both older than the problem."
3. **Triad**: Three items, three clauses or three adjectives because three sounds finished, not because there were three things. 
Example: "Deny-by-default failed loudly, in development, on the safe side."
4. **Dramatic-beat**: A very short sentence or one-line paragraph placed to create a pause or a reveal, when the content doesn't need one. 
Example: "This part I got wrong first."
5. **Closer**: A section or paragraph ending built to land a point: an aphorism, a summary of what was just said, or a line that tells the reader what it all meant. 
Example: "That is the entire argument for it in one incident."
6. **Restatement**: The same point made twice in adjacent sentences, the second adding nothing. 
Example: "Every server that listed a capability could back it up. Nothing advertised tools and then failed to list them."
7. **Cliche-emphasis**: Stock phrases that announce importance instead of showing it, like "the key insight" or "a short shelf life". 
Example: "The difference between those last two is the whole point"
8. **Generic-detail**: A vague phrase where a real detail belongs: a reaction with no content, or a claim about evidence with no evidence. 
Example: "The evidence looked solid."
9. **Reader-instruction**: Telling the reader how to think or react, like "keep in mind" or "sit with that", or a sentence opening on "Consider" or "Imagine". 
Example: "Sit with that for a second."
10. **Hedge**: A universal claim softened so it can't be checked, like "most people" or "plenty of". 
Example: "Plenty of older clients and servers are still around"
11. **Emphasis-word**: Intensifiers that add stress but no information: "exactly", "precisely", "genuinely" and "actually". 
Example: "which is precisely where that one explanatory line went"
